Mathematical Formulations & Filter Kernel Analysis

1. Differencing Equations

• Two-Frame: $\Delta I_t(x, y) = |I_t(x, y) - I_{t-1}(x, y)|$

• Three-Frame: $M_t = (|I_t - I_{t-1}| \ge T) \land (|I_{t+1} - I_t| \ge T)$

• Running Avg: $B_t = (1 - \alpha) B_{t-1} + \alpha I_t$

2. Noise & Filter Formulation

• Gaussian Noise: $\eta \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$, smoothed via $G(i,j) = \frac{1}{2\pi\sigma^2} e^{-\frac{i^2+j^2}{2\sigma^2}}$

• Median Filter: $I_{\text{med}}(x,y) = \text{median}\{I(x+i, y+j)\}$

• Morphology: Opening $(A \ominus B) \oplus B$, Closing $(A \oplus B) \ominus B$

3. Kernel Size Impact (3x3 vs 5x5 vs 7x7)

• 3 × 3: Preserves intricate edge silhouettes, removes 1-pixel noise.

• 5 × 5: Optimal balance for surveillance; merges fragmented vehicle contours.

• 7 × 7: Aggressive cluster elimination, risk of eroding thin limbs/antennas.

4. Evaluation Significance

• Accuracy Limitation: Due to 95% static background pixels, high accuracy is misleading.

• IoU (Jaccard Index): Gold standard overlap ratio $\frac{\text{Area of Overlap}}{\text{Area of Union}}$.

• F1-Score: Harmonic mean balancing false detections ($FP$) and missed detections ($FN$).
